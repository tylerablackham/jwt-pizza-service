# Incident #1 - November 21, 2025

## Summary

Between 16:04 and 16:12 on November 21, 2025, users began encountering problems ordering pizzas. The event was triggered
by a failure on the JWT Pizza Factory. The event was detected by our Grafana Alerting System at 16:07. The team was 
notified at 16:08 and immediately began investigating and working on a solution. This bug affected about 100% of users. 

## Detection

This incident was detected when our Grafana Alerting System was triggered and the JWT Pizza DevOps team was notified. 
Tyler Blackham, who was on call at the time immediately began investigating. 

## Impact

For 8 minutes, between 16:04 and 16:12 on 11/21/2025, 100% of our users began experiencing failures as they ordered
their JWT pizzas. During that time, their orders failed with no explanation.

## Timeline

All times are UTC

- _16:05_ - Users began experiencing failures
- _16:07_ - Grafana Alerting System threshold was reached. 
- _16:08_ - After the pending time, the threshold was officially set to Alert. SMS sent to Tyler Blackham, the on call
technician
- _16:08_ - Incident was acknowledged by Tyler Blackham, who immediately began investigating
- _16:10_ - After searching through the logs, Tyler Blackham discovered the fix and implemented it.
- _16:12_ - Users stopped experiencing failures
- _16:18_ - After verifying that the fix worked and seeing the pizza order failures go back down to zero. Tyler Blackham
marked the incident as resolved.

## Response

After receiving a page at 16:08, on 11/21/2025, Tyler Blackham immediately came online and began investigating

## Root Cause

The root cause was that the JWT Pizza Factory had begun failing.

## Resoultion

The problem was resolved by calling an endpoint `https://pizza-factory.cs329.click/api/support/f5778ce8725c46aeaa0ec7c2e0aad2d4/report/p17rfi4x`
that allowed the JWT Pizza Factory to become fully operation once more.
