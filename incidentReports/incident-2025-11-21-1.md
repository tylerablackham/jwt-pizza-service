# Incident #1 - November 21, 2025

## Summary

Between 1:51 pm and 2:21 pm on November 21, 2025, users began encountering problems ordering pizzas. The event was triggered
by a chaos testing flag being set to true in production at 1:51 pm. The change, meant for testing purposes, made it so
that various orders would fail. The event was detected by our Grafana Alerting System at 2:07 pm. The team immediately 
began investigating and working on a solution. This bug affected about 50% of users. 

## Detection

This incident was detected when our Grafana Alerting System was triggered and the JWT Pizza DevOps team was notified. 
Tyler Blackham, who was on call at the time immediately began investigating. The reason, the incident was discovered 16
minutes after it started occurring was because the threshold for pizza order failures was set to high. The threshold has
since been lowered significantly to allow for quicker discovery of future related incidents

## Impact

For 30 minutes, between 1:51 pm and 2:21 pm on 11/21/2025, 50% of our users began experiencing failures as they ordered
their JWT pizzas. During that time, their orders failed with no explanation.

## Timeline

All times are UTC

- _13:51_ - Chaos flag was set to true. Users immediately began experiencing failures
- _14:07_ - Grafana Alerting System threshold was reached. 
- _14:08_ - After the pending time, the threshold was officially set to Alert. SMS sent to Tyler Blackham, the on call
technician
- _14:08_ - Incident was acknowledged by Tyler Blackham, who immediately began investigating
- _14:16_ - After searching through the logs, Tyler Blackham discovered that the chaos flag was set to true and
immediately set it back to false.
- _14:21_ - After verifying that the fix worked and seeing the pizza order failures go back down to zero. Tyler Blackham
marked the incident as resolved.

## Response

After receiving a page at 2:08 pm, on 11/21/2025, Tyler Blackham immediately came online and began investigating

## Root Cause

The root cause was that the chaos flag had been set to true in the production system.

## Resoultion

The problem was resolved by setting the chaos flag back to false

## Action Items

We will make sure that the chaos flag is not set in production systems again
